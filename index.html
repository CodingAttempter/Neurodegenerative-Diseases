Dementias

Progressive cognitive decline affecting memory, thinking, and daily function. Interactive cards with advanced hover effects.

🧠

Core Symptoms

  • Memory loss (short-term first)
  • Language difficulties
  • Poor judgment
  • Personality changes

Progressive decline affecting daily independence.

🔬

Common Types

  • Alzheimer's (60-80% of cases)
  • Vascular dementia
  • Lewy body dementia
  • Frontotemporal dementia

Each type has distinct pathology and progression.

⚕️

Treatment Approach

  • Symptomatic medications
  • Cognitive therapy
  • Caregiver support
  • Lifestyle interventions

No cure, but progression can be slowed.

Parkinsonism

Movement disorders characterized by bradykinesia, rigidity, tremor, and postural instability.

🦵

Motor Symptoms

  • Tremor at rest (pill-rolling)
  • Bradykinesia (slowed movement)
  • Rigidity (cogwheel pattern)
  • Postural instability

Asymmetric onset typical of Parkinson's.

💊

Causes & Types

  • Idiopathic Parkinson's disease
  • Drug-induced (antipsychotics)
  • Vascular parkinsonism
  • Progressive supranuclear palsy

Distinguished by history, exam, and response to levodopa.

🧬

Dementia Overlap

  • Parkinson's disease dementia (30-40%)
  • Dementia with Lewy bodies
  • Visual hallucinations common
  • Fluctuating cognition

Cognitive decline affects 75% of PD patients over time.

Neurology Timeline

Key milestones in understanding dementias and movement disorders.

1817

James Parkinson publishes "Shaking Palsy" describing the classic symptoms.

1906

Alzheimer describes first case of Alzheimer's disease with plaques and tangles.

1960

Levodopa discovered as effective treatment for Parkinson's symptoms.

1997

Alpha-synuclein identified as key protein in Parkinson's disease pathology.
