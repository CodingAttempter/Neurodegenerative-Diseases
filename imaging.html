Advanced neuroimaging techniques to track disease progression

Visualizing the Brain to Understand Disease

Advanced neuroimaging is a cornerstone of our research efforts. Using cutting-edge technologies like MRI, PET scans, and other advanced imaging tools, we can visualize brain structure and function at unprecedented detail.

These imaging techniques allow us to track disease progression over time, identify biomarkers that indicate early disease, and evaluate how treatments affect brain health and function.

Our imaging research helps us understand how Parkinsonism and Dementias damage the brain and provides critical insights that guide the development of more effective treatments.
