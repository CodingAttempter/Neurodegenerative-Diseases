What You'll Be Doing

Meaningful work with real impact on patients and families

Supporting Patients

You'll work directly with patients receiving treatment, offering emotional support, helping with daily tasks, and being a compassionate presence during medical appointments and therapy sessions.

Helping Families

Connect with family members and caregivers who need guidance, resources, and someone to talk to. You'll help them navigate the challenges of managing neurodegenerative diseases.

Collaborating with Healthcare Teams

Work alongside doctors, nurses, and specialists to understand patient needs and support their treatment plans. You'll be an important bridge between patients and medical professionals.

Spreading Awareness

Educate hospital visitors and community members about Parkinsonism and Dementias, early warning signs, and prevention strategies to help more people seek early diagnosis and care.
