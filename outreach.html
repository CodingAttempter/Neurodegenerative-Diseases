Reaching Communities with Knowledge

Public outreach is fundamental to our mission. We work to educate communities, schools, and organizations about Parkinsonism and Dementias, helping people recognize early warning signs and understand available support resources.

Through workshops, seminars, educational materials, and community events, we bring awareness directly to the people who need it most - patients, families, caregivers, and healthcare providers.

By sharing knowledge and resources, we empower communities to take action, seek help early, and support those living with these diseases.
